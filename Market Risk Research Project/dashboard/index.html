Portfolio construction

SPY only holds 100% SPY. Equity mix targets one-third each in SPY, QQQ and IWM. Stocks, bonds & gold targets 20% in each of the five ETFs.

Weights drift with returns. Monthly portfolios reset at the start of a new month using only earlier holdings. Buy-and-hold portfolios do not reset. No portfolio weights are fitted to the results.

Trading costs

A basis point is 0.01%. Costs are applied to the total amount bought and sold, including the initial purchase. Net daily return is (1 − cost) × (1 + gross return) − 1. Taxes, market impact and final liquidation are excluded.

Metrics

Returns compound. Annualized return uses 252 trading days per year. Volatility is sample standard deviation × √252. Sharpe uses mean excess daily return divided by daily standard deviation, with an assumed 0% risk-free rate.

Drawdown measures the fall from an earlier peak, including the starting investment. Filtered windows reset the reference peak. Results with fewer than two daily observations are not displayed.

Limitations

Historical results do not establish future performance. This is a selected five-ETF universe. Yahoo adjusted closes are accepted as supplied; the audit does not independently verify them. Comparing source dates cannot detect a date missing from every ETF.
