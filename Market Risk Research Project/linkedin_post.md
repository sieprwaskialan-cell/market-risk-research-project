I've been working on a market risk project with Conor McMillan alongside my Maths with Economics degree.

We looked at whether adding bonds and gold to an equity portfolio reduced risk, and what happened to returns.

Using data from 2018 to 2026, the mix with bonds and gold had about 36% lower volatility than the equity-only mix, but also a lower annualized return. It still lost about 21% in 2022.

The project uses Python and SQL, with a dashboard to compare the portfolios across different periods and trading costs. The results depend on the dates and assumptions used.
